About Me

Background

As you may have seen throughout this site, my name is Jamil Chuc. I was born and raised in Waukegan, Illinois, and I am proud to call it home. I graduated Summa Cum Laude from Waukegan High School, where I had the honor of serving as President of my school’s National Honor Society and as the Battalion Command Sergeant Major of the Bulldog Regiment.

I am a first-generation college student, and my journey to this point is deeply rooted in my family and my heritage. Both of my parents are from Belize, and I carry my Belizean heritage very close to my heart. Growing up, my household consisted of my parents, my older sister, and me. My family and friends have always been a major part of who I am, and their support, sacrifices, and love continue to influence everything I do.

Now, I am excited to begin the next chapter of my journey at the University of Michigan, where I will be attending the College of Literature, Science, and the Arts. As I take this next step, I hope to build upon everything I have learned from my family, my community, and my experiences in Waukegan. I am eager to challenge myself, explore new opportunities, and grow both academically and personally while staying true to the people and values that brought me here.

What I'm Up to Now

As a current college student at UofM, I am pursuing a Double-Major in Data Science and Economics. I am a member of the Michigan Community Scholars living community, where my fellow scholars and I participate in community service opportunities across Washtenaw County. I am also a Summer Bridge Alum, and a member of the Comprehensive Studies Program here at UMich.

In my free time, I have taken in upon myself to learn different ways to apply what I am learning in school. I plan to work on coding projects, just like this website. I am also learning to experience my new college life in Ann Arbor. As time passes, I hope to use my time balancing productivity and sociabilty in my advances to one day being a Data Analyst.
